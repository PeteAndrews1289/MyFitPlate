# MyFitPlate Support

For account, privacy, billing, data, or technical support, email **peteandrews1289@gmail.com**.

Include a short description of the problem, the app version, and the device model. A screenshot is
helpful when it does not contain information you do not want to share. Never send your password,
full health export, or another person's personal information.

## Common Tasks

- **Review or correct food data:** Open the food's **Trust Receipt** to inspect its sources and
  nutrition checks. Use **Fix data** to correct a questionable value before saving or logging it.
- **Correct a logged food or workout:** Open the entry from the Daily Log or training history and
  use its edit or delete action.
- **Remove a generated meal plan:** Open **Meal Plan**, choose the menu button, and select
  **Discard Plan**. This removes the generated seven-day plan and its generated grocery items while
  preserving manually added grocery and pantry items.
- **Refresh Apple Watch data:** Open MyFitPlate on both iPhone and Apple Watch. On the Watch, use
  the sync button when it appears. Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled and confirm the Watch app is
  installed from the Watch app on iPhone.
- **Use Maia Read Aloud:** Choose a voice in Maia's voice settings. When the optional online voice
  is unavailable or AI sharing is disabled, MyFitPlate uses the selected Apple system voice on the
  device instead.
- **Troubleshoot an AI or camera feature:** Confirm **Settings > AI data sharing** is enabled, then
  retry on a stable connection. Camera and text estimates always open for review before they are
  added to the diary.
- **Change Apple Health access:** Open MyFitPlate Settings and choose **Review Health access**, or manage MyFitPlate in the iOS Health app.
- **Change AI data sharing:** Open **Settings > AI data sharing**. Apple Health context is optional and controlled separately within that screen.
- **Export data:** Open **Settings > Export your data**.
- **Delete an account:** Open **Settings > Delete account** and verify your password. The app reports success only after account data and the login record have been deleted.
- **Reset a password:** Use the password-reset option on the MyFitPlate sign-in screen.

MyFitPlate does not provide emergency or medical services. Contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.
