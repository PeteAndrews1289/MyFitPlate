# MyFitPlate 2.4

- Find branded foods by product name, flavor, and package size. Tap the plus button to open food logging, review the portion, and save it to the day you selected.
- When equipment is busy, choose **Do Later** and return to that exercise during the same workout. Your completed sets stay recorded. Finishing a program workout saves it to history and advances the program to its next session.
- Start a cooking timer directly from a recipe instruction and choose the duration when the recipe gives a range. Tap its alert to reopen that timer and read the step you started it from.
